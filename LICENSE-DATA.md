# Dataset terms

Dataset coverage: the strict and recovery raw sets (main, no-smoothing and no-standoff
pairs; `data/README.md`), the selection files under `data/selections/` and the
grasp-candidate database `assets/grasp_candidates/retractor_rake.json`. They
hold joint trajectories, grasp poses, wrist-camera renders (tool-masked RGB,
depth, masks) and metadata; no source file, mesh, collision part, USD, texture
or point cloud of the third-party tool asset.

Dataset download links will be released upon publication. The license, to be
chosen by the authors, covers their own contribution and grants no rights to the
third-party asset the data was generated with, which is obtained separately
(`THIRD_PARTY_NOTICES.md`, `docs/ASSETS.md`).
