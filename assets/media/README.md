Both external-camera demonstrations retain their complete selected recordings.
The combined view is the selected 98-second excerpt, starting nine seconds into
its source recording. All three play at the original speed and 30 frames per
second. The videos show a robot and surgical phantom, not patient procedures.
They illustrate behavior and are not a quantitative success-rate evaluation.

For practical repository size, the videos use H.264, 720-pixel height, CRF 27,
limited peak bitrate, and MP4 fast-start metadata. These are presentation copies;
lossless camera data and metric depth are not included in this media folder.
Colors and overlays in the combined view are visualizations, not depth data.
